Openzeppelin provides ERC20Burnable that allows:
    - token.burn(<amount>)
Burns <amount> tokens of the tx caller.

    - token.burnFrom(<contract address>, <amount>)
Requires allowance approval first (approve()).
Burns tokens from the token owner account.

  - Copy ERC20Burnable from Openzeppelin.
  - Create a browine 'burnApp'.
     -- Load, compile, and deploy ERC20Burnable.
  - Create scripts:
     -- Deploy the contract:
	 MTK is the token symbol.
         Initial mint is 1000 MTK.
	 Let 'me' be the contract owner (who deloys the contract).
     -- script1.py for burn().
	 Let 'john' be a normal account.
         Transfer 100 MTK from 'me' to 'john'.
         'john' burns his own 10 MTK using burn().
         Show 'john' MTK balance and MTK totalSupply.
     -- script2.py for burnFrom().
	 Let 'jack' be a normal account.
         'me' approves 'jack' to spend 200 MTK.
         'jack' burns 20 MTK using burnFrom().
         Show 'jack' MTK balance and MTK totalSupply.
